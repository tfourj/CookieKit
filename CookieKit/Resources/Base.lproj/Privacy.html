Privacy policy

CookieKit does not collect your data

Effective July 31, 2026

Cookie processing

When you explicitly open the Safari extension, CookieKit asks Safari for cookies that apply to the current http or https page. CookieKit formats those cookies in memory and does not save them.

No collection or tracking

CookieKit has no developer server, analytics, advertising, telemetry, accounts, or third-party SDKs. It does not transmit cookie data or browsing activity to the developer or any third party.

Show cookies

Cookie names and values appear only when you choose Show cookies. The table stays inside the open extension popup, is not transmitted, and is discarded when the popup closes. Anyone who can see your screen or a screenshot may be able to read those values.

Export and copy

Cookie data leaves CookieKit only when you choose Export cookies or Copy cookies. The destination you select may store or process that data under its own privacy terms and is outside CookieKit’s control.

Your control

Safari controls whether CookieKit can access each website. You can change or revoke that access at any time in Safari’s extension settings.

Treat exports like passwords. Cookies can grant access to signed-in accounts. Export them only when necessary, protect the resulting file, and share it only with trusted destinations.
